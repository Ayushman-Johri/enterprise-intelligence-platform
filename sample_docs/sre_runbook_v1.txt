SRE RUNBOOK v1 (published January 2026)

Section 1 - Escalation
Severity 1 incidents must be escalated to the on-call engineering manager within 15 minutes.
The incident commander is the on-call SRE.

Section 2 - Database
Default connection pool size for all services is 50 connections.
Maximum allowed database connections per PostgreSQL cluster: 500.

Section 3 - Backups
Production databases are backed up every 24 hours. Backups are retained for 30 days.

Section 4 - Certificates
Certificates must be renewed at least 30 days before expiry.
