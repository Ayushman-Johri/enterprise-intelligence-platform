ROSMERTA TECHNOLOGIES - TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY (v4, effective April 2026)

1. Hotel: maximum INR 12,000 per night in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru).
2. Meals: maximum INR 2,000 per day.
3. Client entertainment: maximum INR 20,000 per event and requires pre-approval from the department head.
4. Employees may NOT approve their own claims.
5. Each receipt number may be claimed only once.
6. Claims for amounts just below an approval threshold on repeated occasions must be flagged for audit.
