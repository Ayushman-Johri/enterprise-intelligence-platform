SRE RUNBOOK v2 (published June 2026)

Section 1 - Escalation
Severity 1 incidents must be escalated to the on-call engineering manager within 5 minutes.
The incident commander is the on-call SRE; a communications lead must be assigned for customer-facing outages.

Section 2 - Database
Default connection pool size for all services is 40 connections, with a hard minimum of 10.

Section 3 - Backups
Production databases are backed up every 6 hours. Backups are retained for 35 days.
